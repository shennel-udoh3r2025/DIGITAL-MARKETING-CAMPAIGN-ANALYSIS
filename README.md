# NOVAMART DIGITAL-MARKETING-CAMPAIGN-ANALYSIS
Since its launch in 2022, NovaMart has positioned itself as a trusted player in the Nigerian e-commerce market. 
With stiff competition from both local and international platforms, NovaMart differentiates itself through affordable pricing, diverse product offerings, and targeted digital marketing campaigns across platforms like Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Google Ads.
This project focuses on analyzing NovaMart’s digital marketing performance dataset. Specifically, the scope covers: 
- Campaign performance analysis across platforms. 
- Customer segmentation based on demographic attributes.
- Sales attribution to digital campaigns.
- Identification of trends for budget optimization.
